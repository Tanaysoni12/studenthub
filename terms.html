Terms & Regulations

These rules govern student conduct, academic integrity, and college responsibilities.

1. Admission and Enrollment

Students must maintain valid enrollment and keep their records updated. Admission is subject to approval by the college administration and fulfillment of required academic conditions.

2. Attendance Requirement

All students are expected to attend classes regularly. Minimum attendance requirements must be met to be eligible for examinations and internal assessments.

3. Academic Integrity

Cheating, plagiarism, impersonation, or any form of academic dishonesty is strictly prohibited. Violations may lead to disciplinary action, cancellation of marks, or suspension.

4. Discipline and Conduct

Students must respect faculty, staff, and fellow students. Any act of violence, harassment, vandalism, or misconduct is punishable under college disciplinary rules.

5. Dress Code and Campus Behavior

Students must follow the college dress code and maintain proper decorum in classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and campus events.

6. Use of College Facilities

All college resources, including labs, library, internet, and hostels, must be used responsibly. Damage or misuse of property will attract penalties.

7. Examination Policies

Students must appear for examinations as per the official schedule. Late entry, use of unfair means, or missing exams without valid reasons will be considered violations.

8. Leave and Absence

Any leave request must be submitted in advance and supported by valid reasons. Unauthorized absence may affect attendance and academic standing.

9. Event Participation

Participation in college events, competitions, and seminars is encouraged, but students must follow all event rules, deadlines, and code of conduct.

10. Privacy and Portal Use

Student information in the portal is confidential. Sharing personal credentials or misusing academic records may lead to restrictions on portal access and disciplinary action.

11. Amendment of Rules

The college reserves the right to revise or update these regulations from time to time as required for academic excellence and institutional discipline.
